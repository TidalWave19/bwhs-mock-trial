THE THREE CHAPTERS

Three chapters. One verdict.

01

The Argument

Mock trial is competitive advocacy. We take a real case packet, research the facts, build a theory, and train until every opening, direct, cross, and closing is courtroom ready.

02

The Evidence

Preparation is our whole deal. We study witness statements, exhibits, and impeachment material, then practice until every question has a purpose.

03

The Verdict

The goal is not just winning a trophy. It is learning to advocate, think on your feet, collaborate, and represent your school with confidence.
